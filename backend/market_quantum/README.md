## 🌟 Why Market Quantum Co-Pilot?

Standard Large Language Models (LLMs) hallucinate numbers, misinterpret earnings reports, and suffer from emotional revenge trading when plugged into financial APIs. 
**Market Quantum Co-Pilot** wraps AI agents in a **mathematical state machine**:

1. **4-Agent Weighted Consensus (`core/multi_agent_ensemble.py`):** Combines Macro, Order Flow, Cross-Asset, and Technical Momentum with asymmetric veto thresholds ($|S_{total}| \ge 0.70$).
2. **Deterministic Fact-Checking Gate (`core/anti_hallucination_gate.py`):** Enforces zero tolerance for discrepancies between official raw data (SEC EDGAR, FRED, EIA, Binance) and LLM-generated summaries.
3. **Volume Profile Analysis (`core/volume_profile_engine.py`):** Computes Point of Control (POC), Value Area High (VAH), and Value Area Low (VAL).
4. **Stochastic Monte Carlo Risk Engine (`core/monte_carlo_simulator.py`):** Runs 1,000 statistical iterations to calculate Value at Risk (VaR 95%, VaR 99%) and Maximum Drawdown.
5. **Anti-Panic & Mental Guardian (`core/anti_panic_guard.py`):** Automatic -3% portfolio kill-switch, 60-minute revenge-trading cooldown, and rigid 1% capital risk sizing.
